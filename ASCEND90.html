Today's focus

Minimum viable day

On difficult days: eat nourishing meals, move gently if appropriate, take prescribed care seriously, avoid emotional checking, and protect sleep. A recovery day is not a failure.

Weekly training split

Four strength sessions, one easy cardio day, one optional mobility/social movement day, one recovery day. Progress slowly and modify for symptoms.

Progressive overload protocol

Start around 2–3 reps in reserve. Work in the given rep range; once you hit the top of the range with controlled technique across all sets, add the smallest practical weight increment. Keep 1–2 rest days between hard sessions for the same muscle group.

Weeks 1–2: establish baseline and tolerance. Weeks 3–5: add reps or modest load. Week 6: reduce volume if fatigue is accumulating. Weeks 7–10: progressive build. Week 11: lighter week if needed. Weeks 12–13: consolidate and test rep improvements, not maximal lifts.

Financial command center

All balances are editable. Enter current figures; older estimates are not treated as live balances. Debt paid = baseline total minus current total.

Set your debt baseline

Do not count transfers between accounts as debt repayment. Track net debt, minimum payments, interest and any family-loan commitments.

Weekly money ritual · Sunday (30 minutes)

1. Review every balance, due date and transaction.
2. Confirm minimum payments and family-loan payment schedule.
3. Calculate actual cash available after essentials and a small buffer.
4. Put surplus toward the highest-interest debt, unless a different agreed repayment structure applies.
5. Log one career action: strategic conversation, achievement document, application or skill practice.

90-day targets: zero missed payments, 13 money reviews, a documented debt trend, and at least 12 meaningful career-growth actions. Choose a debt reduction amount after reviewing your real cash flow.

Mental reset protocol

Morning (10 minutes)
Daylight, slow start, choose one priority. Avoid checking your ex or dating apps for reassurance.
During work
One 45–60 minute focused block; single task, notifications off, short break afterward.
After work
Move or train appropriately, eat, connect with a friend, do something enjoyable.
Evening (15 minutes)
Write: What did I feel? What helped? What matters tomorrow? Wind down before bed.

If you feel compelled to contact or check Gavin: wait 20 minutes, label the emotion, change environment, contact a friend if needed, and decide later from your values rather than urgency.
